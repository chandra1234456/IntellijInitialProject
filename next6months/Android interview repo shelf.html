Jetpack Compose — What Interviewers Probe

Compose questions rarely stop at "what is a composable" — they probe recomposition, state hoisting, and performance.

Recomposition
When exactly does a composable re-run, and how do you prove it with the Layout Inspector or recomposition counts?
State hoisting
Why "stateless composables, stateful callers" is the pattern, not just a style preference.
remember vs rememberSaveable
What survives recomposition vs configuration change vs process death — and why the difference matters.
derivedStateOf
Avoiding unnecessary recomposition when a value is computed from other state.
Side effects
LaunchedEffect vs SideEffect vs DisposableEffect — when each fires and what leaks if you pick wrong.
Compose vs View interop
AndroidView / ComposeView — how a legacy screen and a new Compose screen coexist during migration.

Recomposition, precisely
What triggers it, what state to hoist to avoid it firing unnecessarily.
A real offline-first trade-off

Where your sprint fits SDLC
